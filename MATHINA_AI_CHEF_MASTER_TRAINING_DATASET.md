## 4. Master Chef Recipes & Step-by-Step Cooking Guide

### Recipe 1: Master Dum Biryani (Ambur / Muslim Wedding Style)
- **Recommended Cut:** Mathina Big Curry Cut / Biryani Cut (1 kg)
- **Prep Time:** 45 mins | **Cook Time:** 35 mins | **Servings:** 4–5 people

#### Ingredients Table
| Ingredient | Quantity | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mathina Biryani Cut Chicken | 1000g (1 kg) | Juicy bone-in meat retains moisture |
| Aged Seeraga Samba or Basmati Rice | 750g | Parboiled with whole spices |
| Thick Curd / Yogurt | 200g | Lactic acid tenderizer |
| Fried Onions (Birista) | 3 large onions, deep-fried golden | Rich sweetness and deep aroma |
| Ginger-Garlic Paste | 3 tbsp (freshly ground 1:1) | Aromatic base |
| Fresh Mint & Coriander | 1 cup each, chopped | Signature freshness |
| Pure Desi Ghee & Oil | 4 tbsp Ghee + 3 tbsp Oil | Moisture & silkiness |
| Whole Spices (Elachi, Lavang, Dalchini, Bay Leaf) | 4 pods, 4 cloves, 2 sticks, 2 leaves | Infused in water and rice |
| Kashmiri Chilli + Biryani Masala | 2 tbsp + 1.5 tbsp | Vivid color and balanced heat |
| Saffron infused in warm milk | 1 pinch in 50ml warm milk | Golden color and floral fragrance |

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Marination (Crucial):** In a large bowl, mix Mathina Biryani Cut chicken with curd, ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, biryani masala, half of the fried onions, half of the mint-coriander, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp oil, and 1.5 tsp salt. Marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight in fridge).
2. **Parboiling Rice:** In a large pot, bring 3 liters of water to a rolling boil with whole spices, 2 tbsp salt, and 1 tsp ghee. Add soaked rice. Cook for exactly 5–6 minutes until 70% done (rice should bend but still have a firm grain in the center). Drain immediately.
3. **Cooking the Chicken Base:** Heat 2 tbsp ghee in a heavy-bottomed handi. Add marinated chicken and cook on high heat for 6–8 minutes until chicken releases its juices and oil separates.
4. **Dum Layering:** Reduce flame to minimum. Evenly spread the chicken at the bottom. Layer the 70% parboiled rice gently over the chicken.
5. **Garnishing:** Scatter remaining fried onions, chopped mint, coriander, drizzle saffron milk, and top with 2 tbsp fragrant desi ghee.
6. **Dum Cooking (Steam Seal):** Cover with a tight lid sealed with aluminum foil or dough. Place a heavy iron tawa underneath the handi. Cook on low flame for 25 minutes.
7. **Resting & Serving:** Turn off heat. **Let it rest unopened for 10 minutes** so juices redistribute. Fluff gently from the sides with a flat spatula. Serve piping hot with onion pachadi (raita) and spicy brinjal salna!

> 💡 **Chef Pro-Tip:** Never stir biryani vigorously from the center or you will break the delicate grains of rice. Always lift and fold from the edge of the pot.

### Recipe 2: Restaurant-Style Chicken Hakka Noodles
- **Recommended Cut:** Mathina Julienne Cut (250g – 300g)
- **Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Cook Time:** 6 mins | **Servings:** 2–3 people

#### Ingredients Table
| Ingredient | Quantity | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mathina Julienne Chicken Strips | 250g | 2mm strips flash-cook in 3 mins |
| Hakka Egg or Wheat Noodles | 200g | Boiled 80% al dente |
| Garlic (minced) | 2 tbsp | Pungent stir-fry aroma |
| Vegetables (Cabbage, Carrot, Bell Peppers, Spring Onion) | 1.5 cups shredded | Crunchy wok contrast |
| Dark Soy Sauce | 1.5 tbsp | Deep umami color |
| Red Chilli Sauce + Green Chilli Sauce | 1 tbsp each | Tangy heat |
| White Vinegar | 1 tsp | Balances soy sodium |
| Black Pepper Powder | 1 tsp | Sharp street-style pungency |
| Cornflour + Egg White | 1 tsp cornflour + 1 tbsp egg white | Velveting chicken meat |

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Velveting Chicken:** Toss Mathina Julienne strips with 1 tsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp black pepper, pinch of salt, 1 tbsp egg white, and 1 tsp cornflour. Rest 10 minutes. This velveting technique locks in moisture and prevents lean breast meat from drying out.
2. **Flash-Searing Meat:** Heat 2 tbsp oil in a smoking-hot carbon steel wok. Drop chicken strips in a single layer. Sear on high heat for 2.5 to 3 minutes until white and just cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Wok Toss Vegetables:** In the same smoking wok, add 1 tbsp oil and minced garlic. Sauté 30 seconds until fragrant. Add shredded cabbage, carrots, and capsicum. Toss continuously on highest flame for 90 seconds (vegetables must stay crunchy!).
4. **Combine & Season:** Add the boiled al dente noodles and cooked chicken back into the wok. Pour dark soy sauce, chilli sauces, vinegar, and black pepper around the outer rim of the wok (the sauce caramelizes against the hot metal for authentic wok-hei).
5. **High-Heat Toss:** Using two spatulas or tongs, toss everything vigorously for 2 minutes on high heat until the noodles are uniformly coated and steaming.
6. **Finish:** Throw in freshly chopped spring onion greens. Serve immediately with chilli vinegar and tomato ketchup.

> 💡 **Chef Pro-Tip:** Cook noodles in batches if you have a domestic stove. Overcrowding the wok drops the pan temperature, creating soggy steamed noodles instead of smoky stir-fried noodles!

### Recipe 3: Velvety Murgh Makhani (Authentic Delhi Butter Chicken)
- **Recommended Cut:** Mathina Boneless Cube Cut (500g)
- **Prep Time:** 30 mins | **Cook Time:** 25 mins | **Servings:** 3–4 people

#### Ingredients Table
| Ingredient | Quantity | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mathina Boneless Cubes | 500g | Tender 1-inch breast/thigh cubes |
| Ripe Roma Tomatoes | 6 large (pureed smooth) | Rich silky makhani base |
| Cashew Nut Paste | 15 cashews soaked & blended | Natural creaminess & thickening |
| Amul Butter | 50g (3 tbsp) | Decadent buttery mouthfeel |
| Fresh Dairy Cream | 3 tbsp | Glossy finish |
| Kasuri Methi (Fenugreek Leaves) | 1 tbsp, toasted and crushed | Signature restaurant aroma |
| Kashmiri Degi Mirch | 2 tbsp | Vibrant red hue without scorching heat |
| Ginger-Garlic Paste | 2 tbsp | Aromatic foundation |
| Honey or Sugar | 1 tsp | Balances tomato acidity |

#### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Tandoori Marinade:** Mix chicken cubes with 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli, 1 tsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp hung curd, and salt. Marinate 30 mins.
2. **Pan-Charring:** Heat 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Sear chicken cubes on high heat for 5–6 minutes until 80% cooked with light golden char marks. Remove and set aside.
3. **Makhani Gravy:** In a heavy pot, melt 2 tbsp butter. Add ginger-garlic paste, sauté 1 min. Pour in strained tomato puree, Kashmiri chilli powder, and salt. Simmer covered for 12–15 minutes until tomatoes reduce and butter leaves the edges.
4. **Cashew Silkiness:** Stir in the cashew paste and 1/2 cup warm water. Cook for 3 minutes on medium-low flame until gravy becomes velvety.
5. **Add Chicken:** Drop the charred chicken cubes along with any resting pan juices into the gravy. Simmer gently for 5–6 minutes so meat absorbs the makhani sauce.
6. **Finishing Touch:** Stir in 1 tsp honey, crushed roasted kasuri methi, and fresh cream. Turn off the flame. Top with a cube of melting butter and serve with garlic butter naan!

### Recipe 4: Chennai Express Crispy Chicken 65
- **Recommended Cut:** Mathina Chicken 65 Cut (500g)
- **Prep Time:** 20 mins | **Cook Time:** 8 mins | **Servings:** 3–4 people

#### Ingredients & Method
1. **Marinade:** Mix 500g chicken with 2 tbsp thick curd, 1 egg white, 2 tbsp rice flour (for crispiness), 2 tbsp cornflour, 1.5 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder, 1 tsp crushed black pepper, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp lemon juice, and fresh curry leaves. Rest 30 mins.
2. **Frying:** Heat oil to 180°C (350°F). Drop pieces individually without crowding. Fry on medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes until deep golden-crimson and crunchy. Drain on a wire rack.
3. **South Indian Tempering (The Secret):** Heat 1 tsp oil in a pan, crackle mustard seeds, 2 slit green chillies, 2 cloves crushed garlic, and a generous sprig of fresh curry leaves. Toss the fried chicken pieces in this tempering for 30 seconds. Serve with sliced onions and lemon wedges!

### Recipe 5: Spicy Chettinad Pepper Chicken Dry Roast (Milagu Varuval)
- **Recommended Cut:** Mathina Small Curry Cut (500g bone-in)
- **Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Cook Time:** 20 mins | **Servings:** 3 people

#### Fresh Chettinad Masala
- Dry roast: 2 tbsp black peppercorns, 1 tbsp fennel seeds (somph), 1 tbsp cumin seeds, 2 dry red chillies, and 1 cinnamon stick. Grind to a coarse fragrant powder.
#### Method
1. Heat 3 tbsp gingelly (sesame) oil in a heavy iron kadai. Sauté 15 peeled shallots (chinna vengayam), 1 sprig curry leaves, and 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste.
2. Add 1 chopped tomato, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and salt. Cook until mushy.
3. Add 500g Mathina Small Curry Cut chicken. Sear on high heat for 3 minutes until chicken turns opaque.
4. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, and cook for 10 minutes until chicken is tender.
5. Uncover, increase heat, and add the freshly ground black pepper-fennel masala. Sauté vigorously for 4–5 minutes until the gravy reduces to a dark, peppery, aromatic glaze clinging to the chicken pieces. Garnish with coriander leaves!

## 5. Poultry Butchery Science, Safety & Storage Standards

### Critical Temperature Matrix
- **Safe Finished Internal Temperature:** **74°C (165°F)** measured with a digital meat probe inserted in the thickest part (away from bone).
- **Danger Zone to Avoid:** Between **4°C and 60°C (40°F – 140°F)** — raw meat should never sit at room temperature for more than 90 minutes.

### Meat Tenderness Guidelines
- **Resting Period:** Always rest cooked whole birds or chicken breasts for 5–8 minutes under a loose tent of foil before carving. Slicing immediately causes up to 20% moisture loss as internal juices leak out.
- **Bone-In vs. Boneless:** Bone-in meat contains bone marrow and connective collagen that breaks down into gelatin during cooking, resulting in richer sauces and higher moisture retention. Boneless meat cooks twice as fast but must be guarded against overcooking through brine, marinades, or high-heat searing.

### Storage & Shelf Life
- **Fresh Chilled Compartment (0°C – 4°C):** 48 hours maximum from purchase date.
- **Deep Freezer (-18°C):** Up to 3 months when vacuum-sealed or wrapped tightly in freezer-safe airtight containers.
- **Thawing Protocol:** Safely thaw frozen chicken overnight inside the refrigerator or submerged in cold water sealed in a leakproof bag (changing water every 30 minutes). Never thaw in hot water or direct room air.
