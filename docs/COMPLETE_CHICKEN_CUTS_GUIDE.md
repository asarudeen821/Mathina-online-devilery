```

   OR
```

### **2. Browse Cuts:**
```
- Scroll through 24 cut cards
- See 5 columns × 5 rows = 25 cuts (responsive grid)
- Each card shows image, name, cooking time
- Hover to see card scale up
```

### **3. View Details:**
```
- Click any cut card
- Modal opens with:
  • Large image (left side)
  • Full information (right side)
  • Badges (Popular/Premium/Boneless)
  • Cooking time, size, bone content
  • "Perfect for" recommendations
```

### **4. Order:**
```
- Click "Order {Cut Name} Now" button
- Redirects to filtered products
- Add to cart and checkout
```
