## 🎨 Logo Description

### Chicken Character
The logo features a friendly, professional chicken character:
- **Body:** White with light red shading
- **Head:** Round with expressive eye
- **Comb:** Red crest on head (chicken crown)
- **Wattle:** Red chin feature
- **Beak:** Orange/yellow triangle
- **Wing:** Stylized feather detail
- **Tail:** Three feathers
- **Legs:** Orange with feet
- **Overall:** Friendly, approachable, professional

### Brand Elements
- **Circle:** Unity, completeness, protection
- **Red Color:** Energy, passion, excitement (appetite)
- **White:** Purity, cleanliness, freshness
- **Text:** Clear, bold, professional font

**The logo perfectly represents Mathina FreshHub - fresh, quality chicken!** 🐔✅
